Many places, one way of looking: reading the world spatially

Sakib Rahman Siddique Shuvo · a knowledge graph for taking stock, 1998–2027

A floodplain childhood, a river dissertation, field tours in fifty districts, climate internships, enterprise GIS and deep-learning climate models look like separate careers. Laid out as a graph, they run into a small set of lessons. The graph is for seeing where those lessons are well supported, where they are thin, and which way the work is moving.

Click any node to read it and see its route to the centre. Pick a strand to follow one line of the story. Position (above the graph) places everything on two axes, technical ↔ interpretive and physical ↔ social, and traces how the centre of gravity has moved.
